# **🛒 Amazon Sales Dashboard**
### *An Interactive Power BI Dashboard for Sales Performance, Profitability, Regional Analysis, and Demand Forecasting*
The Amazon Sales Dashboard is a comprehensive business intelligence solution developed in Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer purchasing behavior, and shipping trends across different regions in the United States. The dashboard combines historical sales analysis with forecasting to help business stakeholders monitor KPIs, identify growth opportunities, optimize logistics, and make data-driven strategic decisions.

# **📌 Short Description / Purpose**
The Amazon Sales Dashboard provides an interactive overview of Amazon's sales ecosystem by tracking revenue, profit, product categories, customer segments, payment methods, shipping modes, and regional sales performance. It also includes sales forecasting to support inventory planning and future business decisions.
The dashboard enables business managers, sales teams, and executives to identify profitable products, evaluate regional performance, understand customer purchasing patterns, and forecast future sales trends.

# **🛠 Tech Stack**

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

* 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Primary visualization platform used to build the interactive dashboard.
* 📂 **Power Query** – Used for extracting, cleaning, and transforming raw sales data.
* 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Developed KPIs, calculated measures, time intelligence, profit calculations, and forecasting metrics.
* 🔗 **Data Modeling** – Created relationships between orders, customers, products, shipping, and geographic datasets.
* 📈 **Interactive Visualizations** – KPI Cards, Donut Charts, Line Charts, Bar Charts, Filled Maps, Forecasting Visuals, and Region Slicers.
* 📁 **File Formats**

  * `.pbix` – Dashboard development
  * `.png` – Dashboard preview
  * Excel/CSV – Source dataset

# **📂 Data Source**
**Source:** Amazon Sales Dataset (Sample Superstore / E-commerce Sales Data)
The dataset contains transaction-level sales records including:

* Order Date
* Sales
* Profit
* Quantity Sold
* Product Category
* Product Sub-Category
* Customer Segment
* Payment Mode
* Shipping Mode
* Geographic Location (State)
* Region
The dataset was cleaned, transformed, and modeled to generate interactive reports and business insights.

# **✨ Features / Highlights**
## **📍 Business Problem**
Large e-commerce businesses generate millions of transactions across multiple product categories, customer segments, and regions. Business leaders often face challenges in answering key operational questions such as:
* Which regions generate the highest sales and profits?
* Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
* How do monthly sales and profits change over time?
* Which shipping methods are used most frequently?
* Which customer segments drive the highest revenue?
* What are the projected sales trends for future planning?
Without a centralized dashboard, analyzing these metrics requires manual reporting and fragmented data analysis.

# **🎯 Goal of the Dashboard**
The dashboard is designed to:
* Monitor overall business performance using real-time KPIs.
* Analyze sales and profitability across different dimensions.
* Compare regional sales performance.
* Identify top-performing products and customer segments.
* Evaluate shipping and payment preferences.
* Forecast future sales trends for inventory and business planning.

# **📊 Walkthrough of Key Visuals**
## **1. KPI Summary Cards**
The dashboard begins with high-level business metrics that provide an overview of Amazon's sales performance.
Key KPIs include:
* **Total Sales:** **$1.57 Million**
* **Total Quantity Sold:** **22K**
* **Total Profit:** **$175.26K**
These KPIs provide executives with an instant snapshot of business performance.

## **2. Regional Filter**
An interactive region slicer enables users to filter the dashboard by:
* Central
* East
* South
* West
This allows detailed regional performance analysis without creating multiple reports.

## **3. Sales and Profit by State (Map)**
The filled map visual displays sales distribution across U.S. states.
It helps users:
* Identify high-performing markets
* Compare regional demand
* Detect underperforming states
* Support regional sales strategy and expansion planning

## **4. Sales by Segment (Donut Chart)**
This visualization compares revenue generated by different customer segments:
* Consumer
* Corporate
* Home Office
It enables businesses to understand which customer groups contribute most to overall sales.

## **5. Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart)**
The line chart compares monthly sales across multiple years.
It helps identify:
* Seasonal demand
* Revenue growth
* Monthly fluctuations
* Peak sales periods
This supports better inventory and promotional planning.

## **6. Sales by Ship Mode (Bar Chart)**
This visual compares sales across shipping methods such as:

* Standard Class
* Second Class
* First Class
* Same Day
It helps evaluate customer delivery preferences and optimize logistics operations.

## **7. Sales by Category (Bar Chart)**
Sales are categorized into major product groups:
* Office Supplies
* Technology
* Furniture
This enables management to compare category performance and prioritize product investments.

## **8. Sales by Sub-Category**
The sub-category ranking highlights top-selling products including:
* Phones
* Chairs
* Binders
* Storage
* Accessories
* Tables
* Paper
* Furnishings
* Machines
This helps identify high-demand products and supports merchandising decisions.

## **9. Sales by Payment Mode (Donut Chart)**
The payment analysis compares customer purchasing behavior across payment methods such as:
* Cash on Delivery (COD)
* Online Payment
* Cards
These insights assist businesses in optimizing payment options and improving customer convenience.

## **10. Monthly Profit Trend**
The profit trend visual tracks profitability over time, allowing stakeholders to compare profit growth with revenue trends and identify periods of higher operational efficiency.

## **11. Sales Forecasting Dashboard**
The second report page uses Power BI forecasting to predict future sales based on historical order data.
The forecast visualization includes:
* Historical sales performance
* Predicted future sales
* Confidence intervals

# **📈 Business Impact & Insights**

### **Revenue Performance Monitoring**
The dashboard provides executives with a centralized view of sales, profit, and order volume, enabling faster and more informed decision-making.

### **Regional Business Optimization**
State-level analysis helps identify high-performing markets and regions with growth potential, allowing businesses to allocate resources more effectively.

### **Inventory Planning**
Category and sub-category performance analysis supports inventory optimization by highlighting products with consistently high demand.

### **Customer Segmentation**
Understanding purchasing behavior across customer segments enables targeted marketing campaigns and personalized customer engagement strategies.

### **Logistics Improvement**
Shipping mode analysis reveals customer delivery preferences, helping businesses improve fulfillment efficiency and reduce operational costs.

### **Demand Forecasting**
Sales forecasting allows management to anticipate future demand, optimize inventory levels, and reduce stock shortages or overstock situations.

# **🔍 Key Insights**

* The business generated **$1.57 million in total sales**, sold **22,000 units**, and achieved **$175.26K in profit**, indicating strong overall performance.
* The **Consumer** segment contributes the largest share of sales, making it the primary customer base for revenue generation.
* **Office Supplies** leads product category sales, while **Phones**, **Chairs**, and **Binders** rank among the highest-performing sub-categories.
* **Standard Class** is the most widely used shipping method, highlighting customer preference for cost-effective delivery options.
* Monthly sales and profit trends reveal seasonal fluctuations, providing valuable insights for promotional campaigns and inventory planning.
* Geographic analysis highlights regional differences in sales performance, enabling focused market expansion and sales optimization.
* Built-in forecasting empowers decision-makers to anticipate future demand, improve supply chain planning, and support long-term business growth through data-driven strategies.
